5. What it is. Maestro's iOS driver is one XCTest test. Its view-hierarchy
   request reads the status bar; when that read meets a remote element of an
   InputUI scene in the very milliseconds SpringBoard removes the scene, XCTest
   fails the query with kAXErrorInvalidUIElement, which fails the test and ends
   the driver; every later flow is DeviceUnreachableException. On flow 66
   (3b38601) the scene was the password sheet's; on flow 54 (d74e9d2) it was
   the keyboard's, whose scene goes some seconds after it hides, after every
   sign-in. The race is in Maestro's driver (2.11.0; nothing on it in its
   changelog up to 2.12.1). No flow can take it out - not with waits, not with
   repeats - and none is changed for it. The supervisor survives it: every flow
   failed after the first lost driver is run once more on its own (79f80b8).
