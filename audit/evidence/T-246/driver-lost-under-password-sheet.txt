written for d74e9d2, named the "Save Password?" sheet as the cause and its

runs the flows lost after it once more. Sections 4 and 5 are corrected below;
the log extracts stand.

   The sheet's InputUI scene went away while the driver read the status bar:
   not flow 66 or its screen. The 21 flows are not unstable - none of them ran

   Not the cause, as first written here: the cause is a race inside Maestro's
