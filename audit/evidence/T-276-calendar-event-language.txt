T-276 - the phone calendar's event in the app's language
=========================================================

== 1. The cause, in the code ==

src/shared/services/calendar/export.ts, eventTitleFor:
    rehearsal.title?.trim() || `Rehearsal: ${rehearsal.projectName}`
src/shared/services/calendar/ownMark.ts, eventNotesFor:
    `Project: ${projectName}\n\nCreated via Rehearsly`
Both literal English; the export never asked which language the app was in.
And eventMatchesRehearsal compared the title only, never the notes, so even a
fixed title would not have rewritten the notes of a rehearsal with its own name.

== 2. The fix ==

- calendarSync.eventTitle / calendarSync.eventNotes in all four dictionaries
  (CalendarSyncTranslations: without them the type does not build). The word
  for a rehearsal is calendar.rehearsal of each language: Rehearsal, Репетиция,
  Ensayo, Probe (the test checks the title starts with it).
- storage.ts appLanguage(): the language chosen in Rehearsly, read from where
  the app keeps it (AsyncStorage 'userLanguage', written by the profile's
  picker via I18nContext and by the account's preferences); English when none,
  as I18nContext starts. Not the phone's language. The key is one constant now,
  shared with I18nContext.
- export.ts eventWords(): translations[await appLanguage()].calendarSync, read
  once per rehearsal at each export (syncOneRehearsal) and by
  createCalendarEvent / updateCalendarEvent when called alone.
- A name the organiser gave is written as it is (eventTitleFor).
- eventMatchesRehearsal compares the notes too: after a change of language the
  next sync finds the event (by its mapping, or by the URL mark on another
  device), sees words that are not the app's, and updates that same event.
- Recognition unchanged and still English: the URL mark first; for an event
  from before the mark, APP_SIGNATURE (English, both app names) in ownMark.ts
  and the English title `Rehearsal: <project>` in findDuplicateEvent
  (legacyTitleFor, a literal - not the English dictionary, so an edit there
  cannot lose old events).
- unusedKeys.test.ts lists the two keys as read by export.ts; sentenceEnd.test.ts
  has sample arguments for them.

Note: an event whose notes the person edited in the calendar is rewritten with
the app's notes at the next sync, as any update already did before.

== 3. Each language read by its editor ==

Read on the standard of T-256/T-261/T-267/T-268: the app's terms, one Spanish
for every country. Title and notes are labels, "Label: value", no closing
period in any language (as in English), no quotes around the project's name.

EN - no edits. Unchanged from what the export always wrote: "Rehearsal:
  <project>", "Project: <project>" / "Created via Rehearsly".
RU - no edits. «Репетиция: Гамлет» - the screens' word (calendar.rehearsal).
  «Проект: Гамлет» / «Создано в Rehearsly»: the usual iOS phrasing; «в
  приложении Rehearsly» considered and rejected as longer with nothing added;
  the name stays in Latin, undeclined.
ES - no edits. «Ensayo: Гамлет» - the screens' word. «Proyecto: Гамлет» /
  «Creado con Rehearsly»: «con» for the tool, the same in Spain and America;
  «Creado en» considered, «con» is the common form; masculine agrees with
  «evento».
DE - no edits. "Probe: Гамлет" - the screens' word. "Projekt: Гамлет" /
  "Erstellt mit Rehearsly": the idiomatic form ("Erstellt in" considered,
  rejected).
