T-276 - the phone calendar's event in the app's language
=========================================================

== 1. The cause, in the code ==

src/shared/services/calendar/export.ts, eventTitleFor:
    rehearsal.title?.trim() || `Rehearsal: ${rehearsal.projectName}`
src/shared/services/calendar/ownMark.ts, eventNotesFor:
    `Project: ${projectName}\n\nCreated via Rehearsly`
Both literal English; the export never asked which language the app was in.
And eventMatchesRehearsal compared the title only, never the notes, so even a
fixed title would not have rewritten the notes of a rehearsal with its own name.

== 2. The fix ==

(As of ea17d36. The title's word order and a named rehearsal's title changed on
18.11: sections 3 and 5.)

- calendarSync.eventTitle / calendarSync.eventNotes in all four dictionaries
  (CalendarSyncTranslations: without them the type does not build). The word
  for a rehearsal is calendar.rehearsal of each language: Rehearsal, Репетиция,
  Ensayo, Probe (the test checks the title starts with it).
- storage.ts appLanguage(): the language chosen in Rehearsly, read from where
  the app keeps it (AsyncStorage 'userLanguage', written by the profile's
  picker via I18nContext and by the account's preferences); English when none,
  as I18nContext starts. Not the phone's language. The key is one constant now,
  shared with I18nContext.
- export.ts eventWords(): translations[await appLanguage()].calendarSync, read
  once per rehearsal at each export (syncOneRehearsal) and by
  createCalendarEvent / updateCalendarEvent when called alone.
- A name the organiser gave is written as it is (eventTitleFor).
- eventMatchesRehearsal compares the notes too: after a change of language the
  next sync finds the event (by its mapping, or by the URL mark on another
  device), sees words that are not the app's, and updates that same event.
- Recognition unchanged and still English: the URL mark first; for an event
  from before the mark, APP_SIGNATURE (English, both app names) in ownMark.ts
  and the English title `Rehearsal: <project>` in findDuplicateEvent
  (legacyTitleFor, a literal - not the English dictionary, so an edit there
  cannot lose old events).
- unusedKeys.test.ts lists the two keys as read by export.ts; sentenceEnd.test.ts
  has sample arguments for them.

Note: an event whose notes the person edited in the calendar is rewritten with
the app's notes at the next sync, as any update already did before.

== 3. Read by the native speakers; the word order decided by the owner ==

The native speakers of ru, en, es and de read the strings of ea17d36. Their
reports, as written: T-276-natives-ru.md, -en.md, -es.md, -de.md.

No errors in any language. The notes (eventNotes) stay as they were in all four.
All four noted that a rehearsal with its own name shows no project in the
calendar. On the order of the words they differ: ru proposed the project first;
en and es found it better when cut short (the narrowest places show the same
"Rehearsal…"/"Ensayo…" on every event) and left it to the owner; de advised
keeping "Probe:" first ("Hamlet…" at 19:30 can read as a ticket to the show) and
gave "Hamlet: Probe" as the form if the owner decided otherwise. The owner
decided (18.11, "Проект первым"): the project comes first, as in the pushes, for
named rehearsals too.

Final titles:

  lang | no name of its own  | with its own name
  -----+---------------------+----------------------------
  ru   | Гамлет: репетиция   | Гамлет: Прогон 2-го акта
  en   | Hamlet: Rehearsal   | Hamlet: Act II run-through
  es   | Hamlet: Ensayo      | Hamlet: Pasada del 2.º acto
  de   | Hamlet: Probe       | Hamlet: Durchlauf 2. Akt

The forms without a name are the ones in the speakers' own reports. The
organiser's name is written as typed: not translated, not quoted.

Not changed: what a person edits in the event itself (notes, time) is put back
by the sync; the rehearsal is the source and the calendar its mirror (CLAUDE.md,
Calendar Sync). No warning goes into the notes (the ru speaker's question).

== 5. The project first (18.11): red on ea17d36, green after ==

The change:
- calendarSync.eventTitle in all four: `${project}: репетиция` (lower case after
  the colon, the ru speaker's choice) / Rehearsal / Ensayo / Probe.
- export.ts eventTitleFor: a named rehearsal is `${project}: ${title}`, the same
  in every language.
- export.ts legacyTitlesFor: an event without the URL mark, with the English
  signature, is known by "Rehearsal: <project>" or by the organiser's name alone,
  which is how the export wrote it before T-276. Without the second, the named
  rehearsal's old event would have been doubled the moment the title changed.
- Events already exported are rewritten in place at the next sync (accepted by
  the owner): eventMatchesRehearsal sees a title that is not the current one.
