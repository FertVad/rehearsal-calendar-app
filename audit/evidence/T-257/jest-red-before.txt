Kyiv twice, reproduced - a phone that reports the zone's current IANA name:

  ● Kyiv, whichever name of the zone the phone has › a phone in Europe/Kyiv sees Kyiv once
    Expected length: 1
    Received length: 2
    Received array:  [{"labelEn": "Kyiv", "labelRu": "Kyiv", "value": "Europe/Kyiv"}, {"labelEn": "Kyiv", "labelRu": "Киев", "value": "Europe/Kiev"}]

The cause: the list compared zone names as strings. Europe/Kyiv is not the
string "Europe/Kiev", so the phone's zone was put in front of the list as a
zone of its own, though Europe/Kiev is an IANA link (alias) of Europe/Kyiv
since tzdata 2022b. Fixed by ALIASES / sameTimezone in timezones.ts, used by
the list, the labels and both pickers' check mark.

The passing guards (✓) hold on old and new code alike: English names, English
search in every language, a phone already on Europe/Kiev.
