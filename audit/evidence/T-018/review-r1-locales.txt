T-018 review r1, [P2]: the form's month threw in Spanish and German.

Cause: DayCalendar (and DateRangePicker) set LocaleConfig.defaultLocale to the
app's language, and profileCalendar.tsx registered words for 'ru' and 'en'
only. The library read getLocale().dayNamesShort of undefined for 'es'/'de'.
Fix (a16d601): calendarLocales is a Record<Language, ...> in profileCalendar.tsx,
so every language of the app has its words and type-check refuses a missing one.
