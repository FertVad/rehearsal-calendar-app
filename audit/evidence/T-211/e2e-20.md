# T-211: flow 20 "Save before the availability is in" red after b17167e

## Cause

`b17167e` (cause fix 22, review) added flow 42 and, in `e2e/seed.mjs`, a second
`charlotta` key in `PEOPLE` — the person already owned by flow 20 — and made her
the admin of Gaev's "Estate Ball". The rehearsal form opens on the newest
project the person administers (`useAddRehearsalForm` `defaultProject`), so
Charlotta's form opened on "Estate Ball" instead of "Charlotta Tricks" and the
flow's first assertion failed. The app is unchanged and right; the seed broke
the rule "a flow gets people of its own" (docs/e2e.md).

Fix: flow 42 gets its own person, Olga (`olga@e2e.test`, `${OLGA_EMAIL}`).
No assertion or timeout changed.
