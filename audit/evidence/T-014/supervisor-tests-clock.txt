Cause - the test read the machine's clock, not the T-014 change
  After Create and OK the form starts over (useAddRehearsalForm.resetForm) at
  the next whole hour of the wall clock with the usual three hours. jest runs
  in America/New_York (jest.config.js). Between 21:00 and 22:59 there the
  default runs past midnight, the form asks for both days' busy time
  (projectsAPI.getMembersAvailabilityRange, cause fix 15), and the test's API
  stand-in had no such function. The supervisor ran at ~04:40 Israel time =
  ~21:40 New York. At any other hour the test passed, which is why it passed
  on T-013 review 2 and in this task's own runs.

Fix (test only, no app code)
  - the suite's clock is the test's: Date is faked at 2026-10-31T02:30:00Z
    (22:30 New York, the hour that failed), timers stay real for settle();
    real timers back after each test;
  - the API stand-in has getMembersAvailabilityRange, answered like
    getMembersAvailability.
