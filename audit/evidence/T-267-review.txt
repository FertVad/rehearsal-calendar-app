== 2. Each language read by its editor ==

Each language was read on its own by an editor working as a native speaker
of it. The standard is the one the app's native speakers set in T-256 and
T-261 (src/i18n/__tests__/proofreadingEdits.test.ts): the same voice and terms
as the app, one Spanish for every country, no "Ensemble" in German, a no-break
space before the German ellipsis, « » in Russian and Spanish, „ “ in German.
Reading the page's words only (server/routes/invitePage.js, TEXT).

EN - editor's verdict: reads naturally; one edit.
  - lead: "when the whole cast is free" -> "when everyone in the project is
    free". "Cast" is a theatre word, the app names the people "participants"/
    "everyone in the project", and a band or choir is not a cast.
  - Kept: "You’ve been invited to a project", "Open in the App Store",
    "Come back to this page and tap “Open invitation”.", the note.

RU - editor's verdict: grammatical, idiomatic; one edit, for the same term.
  - lead: "когда свободна вся команда" -> "когда свободны все участники
    проекта" (the app's "участники").
  - Kept: «Вас пригласили в проект», «Установите Rehearsly из App Store.»,
    «Открыть в App Store», «Вернитесь на эту страницу и нажмите «Открыть
    приглашение».» (quotes as the app's), the note.

ES - editor's verdict: three edits for a Spanish every country reads alike.
  - heading: "Te invitaron a un proyecto" (Latin American preterite; Spain
    says "Te han invitado") -> "Tienes una invitación a un proyecto".
  - lead: "todo el elenco" -> "todos los participantes" (the app's term since
    T-256: "Todos los participantes"; "elenco" is the theatre's only).
  - note: "Pasa directo al paso 2. Al instalarla, la invitación no se abre
    sola" ("directo" as an adverb is regional; "al instalarla" dangled with
    "la invitación" as subject) -> "Pasa directamente al paso 2. Instalarla no
    abre la invitación".
  - Kept: "Instala Rehearsly desde el App Store.", "Abrir en el App Store"
    ("el App Store", as Apple and the landing write it), «Abrir invitación».

DE - editor's verdict: du throughout, as the app and the landing; two edits.
  - lead: "wann das ganze Ensemble frei ist" -> "wann alle im Projekt Zeit
    haben" (T-256 took "Ensemble" out of the app).
  - status: "Rehearsly wird geöffnet …" with a plain space -> a no-break
    space before the ellipsis, so it cannot wrap alone (the app's rule).
  - Kept: "Du wurdest zu einem Projekt eingeladen", "Installiere Rehearsly aus
    dem App Store.", "Im App Store öffnen", „Einladung öffnen“, the note.
