T-407 - green after: password AutoFill not allowed on the e2e simulator (noPasswordOffer, e2e/run.mjs)
======================================================================================================

The change: after boot the runner reads restrictedBool.allowPasswordAutoFill from
the simulator's EffectiveUserSettings.plist; unless it is false, it shuts the
device down, writes allowPasswordAutoFill = false into its UserSettings.plist
(plutil) and boots it again. After that it asserts the effective value is false
(the run stops, not as the machine's failure, if iOS ever stops reading it).
The Settings toggle (com.apple.WebUI AutoFillPasswords) is still written.
