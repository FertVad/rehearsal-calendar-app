on the app giving up after ten. The app's ten seconds are the axios timeout,
which React Native hands to NSURLSession as the request's timeoutInterval
("timeouts(10.0, …)"); iOS is the one to give up. Here it did not: the task
heard nothing for 40.0 s, received the 200 the hold sent when it lapsed, and
the import succeeded. The flow's wait for "Sync error" (40 s from 00:26:34.8)
ended at 00:27:15.2, three seconds after the success alert. In a green run
